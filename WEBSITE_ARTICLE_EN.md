### 🚀 What is Video to Frames?

**Video to Frames** is a modern Windows desktop utility powered by OpenCV and Qt. It automatically scans video files (MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WEBM, and more) and exports frames into crisp image files based on your preferred settings.

Best of all: **No installation is required (Portable)**. You don't need Python, FFmpeg, or extra codecs installed on your machine. Just download the single `.exe` file, double-click to launch, and you are ready to go.

### ⭐ Key Features

#### 1. 4 Flexible Extraction Modes
Tailor the extraction process to your exact needs:
* **Time Interval (Seconds):** Extract a frame every $X$ seconds (e.g., every 1.0 second, every 0.5s, or every 5s).
* **Evenly Distributed Count:** Automatically spread across the entire video duration to extract an exact number of photos (e.g., exactly 50 photos from start to finish).
* **Frame Interval:** Grab a frame every $N$ frames (e.g., every 30th frame).
* **Full FPS (Every Single Frame):** Export every single frame in the video for high-speed continuous motion analysis or animation.

#### 2. Zero Quality Loss (Lossless Output)
* **Lossless PNG:** Save frames in 100% lossless PNG format with perfect pixel reproduction and no compression artifacts.
* **High-Quality JPG:** Need to save disk space? Choose JPG with an adjustable quality slider (defaults to 95% for virtually indistinguishable fidelity).
* **Native Resolution:** If your video is 1080p Full HD, 2K, or 4K Ultra HD, your saved images maintain that exact native resolution (e.g., 3840x2160).

#### 3. Smart Timestamped Filenames
Every exported image is automatically labeled with its exact timestamp:  
`frame_00001_01m24s_350ms.jpg`  
This allows you to know the exact minute, second, and millisecond of the video where that shot was captured.

#### 4. Effortless Drag & Drop
No complicated file menus. Simply drag your video file from Windows File Explorer and drop it directly onto the application window.

#### 5. Bilingual Interface (English & Turkish)
Easily toggle between English (🇬🇧) and Turkish (🇹🇷) with a single click from the top-right corner.

### 💻 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

1. **Load Your Video:** Run `VideoToFrames.exe` and drag your video file into the window (or click *"Select Video File"*).
2. **Choose Your Settings:** Select your desired extraction mode (e.g., every 1 second) and format (JPG or PNG).
3. **Start Extraction:** Click **"Start Extracting Frames"**. A real-time progress bar will keep you updated. Once complete, click *"Open Folder"* to view your photos immediately!
